# Attorney Prep Questions

## Critical Before Filing

- question: What does the operative custody order or decree actually require for notice, consultation, and joint decision-making on nonemergency medical appointments and mental-health referrals?
  - why_it_matters: The entire issue may be an enforceable violation, a clarification problem, or no violation at all depending on the exact order language.
  - what_answer_would_change: Whether the draft should frame this as enforcement, clarification, pattern preservation only, Special Master routing, or no action.
  - what_decision_this_would_affect: Whether to file, preserve, escalate to neutral process, or drop the issue.
  - related_source_ids: [DOC-001]
  - priority: Critical
  - audience: attorney

- question: Do stronger source documents confirm that both children actually had appointments on April 29, 2026, that notice was given for only one child, and that no comparable notice was given for the second child?
  - why_it_matters: Right now the core factual sequence exists only in a Tier 4 intake summary, which is not filing-safe.
  - what_answer_would_change: Whether the issue can be stated as a source-backed chronology or only as an unverified concern.
  - what_decision_this_would_affect: Whether any external accusation, motion language, or enforcement framing is safe.
  - related_source_ids: [DOC-001]
  - priority: Critical
  - audience: either

- question: Was any psychiatric referral actually placed, discussed only, or merely inferred from a later call note?
  - why_it_matters: A mental-health referral materially raises the significance of the event, but the current wording is tentative.
  - what_answer_would_change: Whether the draft can mention a real referral, should mention only a possible referral concern, or should omit it entirely.
  - what_decision_this_would_affect: Relief scope, urgency framing, and whether this is routine notice failure versus a more serious medical decision-making issue.
  - related_source_ids: [DOC-001]
  - priority: Critical
  - audience: either

- question: Was the April 29, 2026 care nonemergency routine treatment, or did Parent A have a plausible emergency or same-day justification for acting without fuller notice?
  - why_it_matters: Emergency facts can defeat or narrow a notice-based complaint and materially affect credibility.
  - what_answer_would_change: Whether counsel frames this as unilateral exclusion, justified medical action, or a narrow communication/process issue.
  - what_decision_this_would_affect: Whether to pursue enforcement, seek clarification, or avoid overreaching.
  - related_source_ids: [DOC-001]
  - priority: Critical
  - audience: either

- question: Does counsel want to pursue a one-off notice violation theory, or preserve this only if it fits a documented broader pattern of unilateral medical decision-making?
  - why_it_matters: A single event may not justify the same relief or tone as a repeated pattern.
  - what_answer_would_change: Whether the draft seeks immediate relief, limited preservation, or no present action.
