# Legal Authority Rules

- Legal propositions require authority.
- Prefer binding primary authority.
- Classify authority as binding_primary, persuasive_primary, secondary, supplied_material, or unverified.
- Jurisdiction matters.
- Currentness/citator status must be stated.
- If currentness is not checked, say so.
- Do not fabricate citations.
- Do not summarize a case holding unless the case text or reliable supplied summary supports it.
- Do not treat secondary sources as binding law.
- Do not pass unsupported legal assumptions to other agents as true.
- If legal authority is missing, produce a research gap or attorney question.
